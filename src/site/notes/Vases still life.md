Stephanie put together this still life arrangement for me to begin at her studio, and I completed it at home. I had another go at sketching it in my sketchbook, and a painted version to capture all those lovely colours.

Working in pencil is great for the detail and isn’t as messy as charcoal, but I found filling the large areas such a pain!
