Hi hi,

If you live in my part of the world (Boorloo, Perth), then the morning are getting brighter, the sun getting towards the bitey-ness of summer, and the weeds have suddenly grown to the height of a person. Or maybe that's just me putting off pulling them up until they started towering over me. I spent some time last weekend attacking the spiky intruders whilst listening to [Masakatsu Takagi](https://takagimasakatsu.bandcamp.com/album/marginalia-110)'s Marginalia, which made me feel like I was in a Studio Ghibli film. If there was ever a mundane domestic chore that needs romanticising to get me through, it's weeding.

![[pulling-weeds.gif|Animation of person pulling up a gigantic weed|300]]
I then had to animate it of course. Frame by frame hand-drawn animation done in Procreate.

Lately I've felt the paintings I make aren't quite turning out the way that I want, which sometimes makes me question the endeavour. But looking back over my past art that I was happy with, I think it's a combination of trying new things that are challenging, plus taste that keeps evolving ahead of my skill level. So these paintings I was disappointed with are a product of continuing to push and grow rather than staying in my comfort zone, and that's not such a bad thing. I just need to take the time to learn from mistakes and keep going.

![[assets/IMG_0158-faf76dfa.jpeg|My Quick Draw watercolour painting in progress]]

One of the paintings I wasn't happy with actually sold - I did the Quick Draw event at [[Plein Air Down Under 2026|Plein Air Down Under 2026]] whilst in Mandurah, and priced it very reasonably (maybe a little too reasonably). It's nice to know that hanging in someone's house! Painting on location is always challenging, and I did learn a lot doing this one.

I do find it interesting to look back and remember that and the start of this year I had never really done a proper painting outside of my sketchbook. I had never exhibited a painting, sold a painting, or made anything with watercolours that I wanted to display in my house. Even with some diversions into knitting, video, animation and web development that are only useful to me, I'm still growing and making progress. And I'm so excited to keep going.
