Another charcoal portrait study, homework again from Stephanie Sheppard. It was an extra challenge drawing from a colour reference rather than one which is already in greyscale - I had to trust my eyes to figure out the values. I really struggled with the nose and mouth and once again had to erase half the face to start over!

![[assets/IMG_9458.jpeg|Portrait in progress]]

Working on this in the mild weather of late winter in Perth, I really enjoyed drawing outdoors in the courtyard, which I like to call my "outdoor studio".
