Calendar Printer

Make a clean, printable calendar — a full year on one page, or one month at a time. Choose a paper size and layout, tune the type and colours, then mark the dates that matter by clicking or dragging straight onto the page.

Prefer typing? Enter ranges in the Highlighting section — Jan 1 yellow, Mar 10-15 green — and they'll paint onto the calendar as you go.

When it looks right, print to PDF or save a PNG. Everything you set lives in the page URL, so bookmark it to keep your setup or share it with someone else.
